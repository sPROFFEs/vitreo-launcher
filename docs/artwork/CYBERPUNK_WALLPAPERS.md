Both images are original AI-generated illustrations, not downloaded MotionBGS videos, official Cyberpunk 2077 artwork, or IconScout animations. Files are the generated PNGs without image edits. Android crops them to cover the viewport. SHA-256 hashes are in `wallpapers.sha256`.

The user's [motorcycle reference](https://motionbgs.com/mobile/cyberpunk-2077-bike), [night-city reference](https://motionbgs.com/mobile/lucy-digital-shadows), and [animation reference](https://iconscout.com/lottie-animation-pack/cyberpunk-essentials-extras-animation-pack_440694) informed the direction. No redistribution grant for those files was established; none are bundled. Ambient rain, light drift, scanner and HUD edge effects are original Compose drawing code, without Lottie, video downloads or network requests. They run only in the opt-in Immersive experience while visible, resumed and focused, and stop for Reduce Motion or Battery Saver. Android uses the same static wallpaper poster during task changes; this is not an Android live-wallpaper service.

## Generation prompts

### Neon Rider

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original portrait Android launcher stock wallpaper, no UI. Primary request: cinematic cyberpunk megacity at night, futuristic motorcycle parked on rain-slick rooftop overlooking dense neon high-rises. Game concept art, highly polished realistic 3D atmosphere. Composition: portrait 9:20, bike and bright horizon confined mostly to lower third, upper two thirds dark airy midnight buildings and mist so app icons remain legible. Lighting: electric cyan reflections, toxic yellow signage-like blocks without readable writing, restrained red brake lights. Deep ink blacks with rich dark blue shadows. Constraints: completely original environment and motorcycle; no game logos, no brand names, no existing characters, no text, no watermark, no phone frame. This is an illustration asset, not an interface mockup.

### Digital Dusk

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original portrait 9:20 stock wallpaper for cyberpunk Android launcher, no interface. Primary request: moody anime cinematic rooftop night scene, a mysterious futuristic female netrunner seen from behind with short white bob hair and dark tech jacket, standing in the lower third looking over vast electric violet and cyan city lights. Completely original character, not a depiction of an existing named character. Composition: quiet midnight navy and purple gradient sky and soft holographic haze across upper two thirds for readable app icons, city skyline and small figure in lower third. Beautiful luminous 2D anime film background, restrained pink highlights, cyan rim light, distant orbital moon. No closeup, no readable text, no logos, no watermark, no frame, no UI, no lettering.
